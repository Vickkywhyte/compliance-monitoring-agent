EBA Consultation Paper on draft ITS on supervisory reporting (EBA/CP/2021/05) — WITHDRAWN

8 March 2021
EBA/CP/2021/05

NOTICE OF WITHDRAWAL. The EBA hereby withdraws Consultation Paper EBA/CP/2021/05 on draft Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) on supervisory reporting, published 8 March 2021. This consultation paper has been superseded by the revised ITS published as EBA/ITS/2024/09 which incorporates the responses received during the consultation and reflects updates to the CRR III framework.

Institutions should disregard the draft ITS contained in EBA/CP/2021/05 and refer instead to the final text in EBA/ITS/2024/09 for compliance planning purposes. The withdrawal is effective from 1 September 2024.

Original scope: The consultation covered draft ITS specifying the formats, frequencies, reference dates, and dates of submission for supervisory reporting requirements applicable to institutions under Article 430 of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013. It proposed 47 new COREP templates and 12 revised FINREP templates. All of this content has been revised and incorporated into EBA/ITS/2024/09.

Institutions that have begun implementation work based on the draft ITS should review EBA/ITS/2024/09 for material differences. Key changes in the final ITS include: (a) revised XBRL taxonomy v4.0 replacing the v3.2 taxonomy proposed in the consultation; (b) extended reporting deadlines for smaller institutions; (c) removal of three draft templates that were deemed disproportionate following consultation feedback.
