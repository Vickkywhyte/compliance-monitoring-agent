EBA Guidelines on internal governance and risk management for AML/CFT (EBA/GL/2024/07)

12 April 2024
EBA/GL/2024/07

These Guidelines specify the internal governance arrangements, processes and mechanisms that credit and financial institutions must have in place to comply with their anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT) obligations under Directive (EU) 2015/849.

The Guidelines are addressed to competent authorities responsible for AML/CFT supervision and to credit institutions, payment institutions, and electronic money institutions operating within the Union. They enter into force on 1 October 2024.

Section 1: Governance body responsibilities

The management body in its supervisory function shall be responsible for: (a) approving the institution's AML/CFT strategy and risk appetite; (b) appointing a dedicated AML compliance officer at senior management level; (c) ensuring adequate resources are allocated to AML/CFT compliance.

Section 2: AML/CFT risk assessment

Institutions shall conduct a comprehensive business-wide AML/CFT risk assessment at least annually. The assessment shall cover: customer risk, product and service risk, delivery channel risk, and geographic risk. Results shall be documented and made available to the supervisory authority on request.

Section 3: Customer risk profiling

Each customer shall be assigned a risk profile (low, medium, or high) based on a documented risk scoring methodology. The risk profile shall be reviewed at least annually and whenever there is a material change in the customer's circumstances. High-risk customers shall be reviewed every six months.

Section 4: Suspicious transaction reporting

Institutions shall establish escalation procedures ensuring that any employee suspicion of money laundering or terrorist financing reaches the MLRO within two working days. The MLRO shall decide whether to file a SAR within five working days of receiving the escalation.

Section 5: Training requirements

All staff in roles with AML/CFT exposure shall complete certified AML training within three months of appointment and refresher training every two years. Training records shall be maintained for five years.

Competent authorities and financial institutions must comply with these guidelines by 1 October 2024.
