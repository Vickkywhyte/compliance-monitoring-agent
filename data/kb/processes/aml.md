# Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

## Overview

The AML process detects and prevents the firm from being used to
launder criminal proceeds. It encompasses transaction monitoring,
suspicious activity reporting, and staff training.

## Risk Assessment

An annual enterprise-wide AML risk assessment identifies the firm's
exposure across products, customers, geographies, and delivery channels.
Business lines update their risk assessments whenever new products are
launched or significant changes occur.

## Transaction Monitoring

Automated transaction monitoring rules are calibrated to detect structuring,
layering, and unusual patterns. Alert thresholds are reviewed quarterly
and documented with statistical justification.

## Suspicious Activity Reporting

Any employee who identifies suspicious activity must file an internal
suspicious activity report (SAR) with the MLRO within 24 hours of
discovery. The MLRO determines whether to submit an external SAR to
the Financial Intelligence Unit within the regulatory timeframe.

## Training

All staff receive AML awareness training annually. Customer-facing staff
and the compliance team receive enhanced training every six months.
