# Regulatory Reporting

## Overview

The regulatory reporting process ensures timely and accurate submission
of all required reports to competent authorities in line with applicable
EU and national regulatory requirements.

## Periodic Reports

Regular reports to supervisory authorities include: COREP capital adequacy
reports (quarterly), FINREP financial reporting (quarterly), large
exposures notifications, and significant institution thresholds.

## Transaction Reporting

EMIR trade reporting requirements mandate that all OTC derivative
transactions are reported to a trade repository. MiFID II transaction
reports must be submitted by T+1.

## Incident Reporting

Material operational incidents, significant cybersecurity events, and
major outsourcing disruptions must be reported to the competent authority
within prescribed timeframes, typically 4 hours for initial notification.

## Record Keeping

All regulatory reports and supporting data must be retained for a minimum
of five years and be available for inspection on request by competent
authorities.
