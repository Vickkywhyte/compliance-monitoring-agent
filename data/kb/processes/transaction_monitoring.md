# Transaction Monitoring

## Overview

Transaction monitoring is the automated and manual process of reviewing
customer transactions to detect patterns indicative of financial crime,
including money laundering, terrorist financing, and sanctions evasion.

## Automated Monitoring

A risk-based automated monitoring system applies scenario-based rules
and statistical models to detect unusual activity. Rules are calibrated
against the customer's expected transaction behaviour and risk profile.

## Alert Management

Generated alerts are reviewed by trained analysts within defined SLAs:
high-priority alerts within 24 hours, standard alerts within 5 business
days. Analysts document their investigation and disposition for each alert.

## Tuning and Calibration

Monitoring scenarios and thresholds are reviewed quarterly and after
any material regulatory change. Back-testing is performed to validate
detection effectiveness. Changes to thresholds require MLRO sign-off.

## Escalation and Reporting

Alerts that cannot be cleared through investigation are escalated to the
MLRO for determination on SAR filing. All alert dispositions are retained
for a minimum of five years.
