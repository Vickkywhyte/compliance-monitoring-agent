# Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

## Overview

Customer Due Diligence is the process of collecting and verifying
information about customers to assess their risk profile and ensure
compliance with AMLD and CRR requirements.

## Standard CDD

Standard CDD applies to all customers and includes: verification of
identity using reliable independent sources, identification of beneficial
owners, and assessment of the nature and purpose of the business
relationship.

## Simplified CDD

Simplified CDD may be applied where there is a demonstrably lower risk,
as defined in the firm's risk appetite. This requires documented
justification and approval from the compliance function.

## Enhanced CDD

Enhanced CDD is mandatory for high-risk customers including PEPs,
customers from high-risk third countries, and relationships where the
risk profile warrants additional scrutiny. Senior management approval
is required for all enhanced CDD relationships.

## Documentation Standards

All CDD documentation must be current, complete, and verified. Digital
verification is acceptable where the technology meets the standards
set by the competent authority.
