# Sanctions Screening

## Overview

The sanctions screening process ensures the firm does not conduct business
with sanctioned individuals, entities, or jurisdictions as required by EU
Council Regulations and national legislation.

## Customer Screening

All new customers are screened against EU consolidated sanctions lists,
UN sanctions lists, and national designations at onboarding. Results are
logged with match scores and reviewed by the compliance team.

## Ongoing Screening

Existing customer records are re-screened against updated sanctions lists
within 24 hours of any list update. Automated alerts are generated for
potential matches requiring analyst review.

## Transaction Screening

All outgoing and incoming payments are screened in real-time against
current sanctions lists before execution. Payments to or from sanctioned
jurisdictions are blocked automatically.

## Escalation

Confirmed sanctions hits trigger an immediate account freeze and
escalation to the MLRO, Legal, and Senior Management. Regulatory
notification is made within the timeframe prescribed by applicable law.
